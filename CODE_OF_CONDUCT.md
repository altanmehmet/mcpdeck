# Community guidelines

MCPDeck welcomes questions, bug reports and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss code and behavior respectfully. Explain disagreements with evidence and reproducible examples.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or personally attack other participants.
- Do not publish another person's private information, account details or credentials.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant. Avoid spam, repeated unsolicited promotion and disruptive behavior.
- Credit others' work and respect the project's license and third-party licenses.

Maintainers may moderate content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportional to the behavior and its impact.

For harassment or abuse, use GitHub's private report/block controls. Do not post private evidence in public threads. Technical vulnerability reports belong in the [private security reporting channel](https://github.com/altanmehmet/mcpdeck/security/advisories/new). General usage questions belong in [Discussions](https://github.com/altanmehmet/mcpdeck/discussions).
